To step back: Patram-Ingest, everything we talked about today, is just one part of the infrastructure we have.

There is a lot supporting it from below: our storage layer, Patram-FS and Patram-DB, the scraper and our synthetic documents that bring raw data in, and DarshinAI for annotation and visualization.

And above it, Patram-Core takes this data, our training corpus, and makes it possible to train models. Using all of this, we have trained many foundation models, the Patram Suite, and evaluated them on our own benchmarks.

These are all internal models as of now. We are soon planning to release the Patram series. If you are interested, please sign up using this form, and we will let you know.
